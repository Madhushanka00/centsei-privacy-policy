# Centsei Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 October 2026
**App:** Centsei (Android package `com.madhushanka.centsei`)
**Developer:** Mahesha Madhushanka ("I", "me", "the developer")
**Contact:** mahesha.madhushanka00@gmail.com

Centsei is a personal money manager that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

## The short version

- Your data stays **on your device**. I do not run servers for Centsei and I never receive your data.
- The app has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- The app does **not need an internet connection** and does not send your data anywhere.
- You can export or erase all of your data at any time.

## Information the app stores

Everything below is created by you (or by features you switch on) and is stored in a local database on your device only:

- Transactions (income, expenses, transfers), amounts, dates and notes
- Accounts, categories, budgets, goals and reminders
- Your display name and app preferences (such as light or dark theme)

I cannot see this information. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, it is deleted from your device.

## Optional: Bank SMS capture (Android only)

If you choose to turn on Bank SMS capture, Centsei can read incoming text messages **only from the sender IDs you add yourself**, to turn bank alerts into transactions.

- Messages from any other sender are ignored immediately and are not stored or logged.
- For allowed senders, the app keeps the sender, message text and received time on your device so it can create the transaction and avoid duplicates.
- The optional catch-up scan reads your inbox only for those senders and never looks back further than 30 days.
- The feature requires the SMS permission, which you can grant or revoke at any time in Android settings. The app works fully without it.
- SMS content is processed on your device and is **never uploaded or shared**.

## Files you import and export

- **Export:** when you export a CSV, the file is created on your device and handed to the share sheet you choose. Where it goes after that is your decision and is governed by the app you share it with.
- **Import:** a file you pick is read on your device and kept only in memory while importing. The app does not copy it anywhere.

## What I do not collect

I do not collect or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, advertising IDs, usage analytics or crash reports. No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services are included in the app.

## Sharing

I do not sell, rent, trade or share your information, because I do not have it.

## Security

Your data is held in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. Your device's own screen lock and encryption protect it further. Because data is not backed up by me, keep your own backups by exporting a CSV from time to time.

## Children

Centsei is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices

- Export your transactions as CSV from More › Settings › Data.
- Erase everything with "Reset data" in the same screen, or by uninstalling the app.
- Turn Bank SMS capture off, or revoke the SMS permission, whenever you like.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding cloud sync), I will update this page and the effective date before the change ships. Previous versions remain available in this repository's history.
